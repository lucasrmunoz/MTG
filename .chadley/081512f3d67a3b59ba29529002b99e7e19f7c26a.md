## Proposal

Replace frame-wide text-line OCR with full-card scanning: detect card quads, warp each flat, read title and collector line per card, and rank artwork to pick the exact printing. One proposal in strict phase order — banded OCR needs the detected quad, artwork ranking needs the warped image, the stability fallback needs per-frame quads.

Attempted 2026-08-05 (commit 9aaf5b1) and reverted the same day (08645eb): every CV threshold shipped blind. Two rules came out of the failure: CV confidence scores rank, never veto; no camera threshold ships without an offline corpus.

Phases: (0) capture-and-calibration harness — ≥50 real-table frames plus a desktop replay harness, the prerequisite the first attempt skipped; (1) OpenCV card-quad detection with dark-surface and rounded-corner fallbacks; (2) per-quad banded OCR pairing title and collector line within one card; (3) dHash artwork ranking for the exact printing; (4, optional) quads as overlay-stability fallback.
