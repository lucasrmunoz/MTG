`GetPricesAsync` filters the incoming ids before making any request:

```csharp
var accepted = printingIds.Distinct().Where(IsAcceptableId).ToList();
```

`accepted` is what actually gets chunked and sent to Mana Pool. But the closing debug log reports against the raw input `printingIds.Count`:

```csharp
logger.LogDebug(
    "Mana Pool returned prices for {Priced} of {Requested} printings.",
    prices.Count,
    printingIds.Count);
```

When duplicates or non-conforming UUIDs are dropped (which `IsAcceptableId` is specifically designed to do), the log claims more printings were "requested" than were ever sent, making the priced/requested ratio look worse than reality and obscuring how many ids were filtered out. Log `accepted.Count` instead (and, if the filtered count matters, it can be surfaced separately), so the "of {Requested}" number reflects what was actually asked of Mana Pool.
