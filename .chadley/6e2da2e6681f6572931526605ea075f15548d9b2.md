Mana costs and reminder text come through as literal Scryfall tokens like `{1}{U}{U}`, `{T}`, `{W/B}`. For a card-lookup tool this is the most visible piece of a card and reads poorly as braces.

**Well-scoped enhancement:** parse the `{...}` tokens in `manaCost` and `oracleText` and render them as the corresponding mana/tap symbols (Scryfall's symbology endpoint provides stable SVGs; a small token→glyph map covers the common set). Scoped to `CardDetail` (and reusable in `PreviewPanel`/faces), no backend change required — the tokens are already present in the data.

Low severity because the current text is legible, but rendered symbols are a large readability win for the app's core view.
