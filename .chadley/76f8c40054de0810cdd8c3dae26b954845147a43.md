`selectedArtUrl` (and the derived `previewVersion`) are never re-validated against the active finish filter, and `previewVersion` is looked up in the **unfiltered** `artVersions` rather than in `visibleVersions`.

Reproduction:
1. Open a card under the "All" finish and click a printing that exists only in non-foil → `selectedArtUrl` points at it.
2. Switch the finish to "Foil".
3. `visibleVersions` now excludes that printing, so it disappears from the grid and can no longer be selected/cleared, and its highlight is gone.
4. But `previewUrl` still equals `selectedArtUrl`, so `previewVersion` still resolves it from the unfiltered list — the **main CardDetail image** (`imageUrl={selectedArtUrl ?? card.imageUrl}`) and the **preview panel** keep showing that printing, with foil styling and a `—` price, while the grid simultaneously reports "No printings … exist in this finish." The two views contradict each other.

**Suggestion:** when `finish` changes, reconcile `selectedArtUrl` (and `hoveredArtUrl`) against `visibleVersions` — e.g. clear/reset the selection to the card's default when the selected printing is filtered out, or resolve `previewVersion` from `visibleVersions` so the image, price, and grid stay consistent.
