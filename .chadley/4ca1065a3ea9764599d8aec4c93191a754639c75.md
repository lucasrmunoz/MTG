`parseGame` validates `startingLife` with only `typeof startingLife !== "number"`. Because `NaN` and `±Infinity` are of type `number`, a corrupt or hand-edited save carrying a non-finite `startingLife` passes the check and is returned verbatim. Unlike the turn counter (guarded with `Math.max(1, Math.trunc(turn))`), `startingLife` is never clamped or coerced, so it surfaces as `NaN` in the setup/summary UI and would poison any future 'reset life' affordance that restores players to `startingLife`. This is a distinct field from the already-tracked non-finite `life`/`commanderCasts` issue.

**Fix:** after the `typeof` check, also require `Number.isFinite(startingLife)` (ideally `startingLife >= 1`), returning `null` otherwise, mirroring the turn-counter guard.
