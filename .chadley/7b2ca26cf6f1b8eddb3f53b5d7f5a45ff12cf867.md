Same class of defect as the CardDetail finding, in the right-hand preview. `foil={finish === "foil"}` and the `· foil` suffix are driven solely by the selected finish, independent of whether the previewed printing has a foil price.

When a non-foil-only printing is previewed under the "Foil" finish, the panel shows the foil sheen and a `TCGplayer (market) · foil` caption, while `formatPrice(price)` renders `—` because `priceFor(..., "foil")` returns `null`. The panel therefore claims a foil view of a card that has no foil printing.

**Suggestion:** apply the foil sheen and `· foil` label only when `price !== null` under the foil finish (or pass an explicit `isFoil` flag computed from the actual price availability).
