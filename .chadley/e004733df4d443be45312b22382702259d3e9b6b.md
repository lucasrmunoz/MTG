`ExecuteAsync` does one immediate refresh, then waits on a fixed 1-hour `PeriodicTimer` (`PriceRefreshService.cs:24,28,36`). `RefreshAsync` swallows every non-cancellation exception and "keeps the previous snapshot" (`PriceRefreshService.cs:63-72`).

That is the right behavior once a snapshot exists, but on cold start there is no previous snapshot. If the very first download fails — plausible, since the Card Kingdom feed is described in its own source as "an undocumented, unsupported feed... no stability guarantee" (`CardKingdomFeed.cs:11-15`) — that vendor's prices are absent for a full hour before the next attempt, degrading the price feature far longer than necessary.

**Fix sketch:** use a shorter retry interval (with backoff) until each feed's first successful load lands, then fall back to the hourly cadence. The `PriceCache`/`IsLoaded` state already distinguishes "never loaded" from "stale," so the condition is cheap to check.
