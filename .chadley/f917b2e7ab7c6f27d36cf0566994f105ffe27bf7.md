The 66 MB Card Kingdom feed is fetched with a plain `fetch` and read chunk-by-chunk with no timeout or `AbortSignal`. The C# equivalent gets a 10-minute `HttpClient.Timeout` (`ServiceCollectionExtensions.PriceFeedTimeout`), but the on-device path has nothing.

Because `inFlight` is only reset once the download promise settles, a connection that opens and then stalls mid-stream wedges the guard permanently: the PriceControls "Loading prices…"/"Refreshing…" spinner never stops, and selecting or refreshing Card Kingdom does nothing thereafter until the app is restarted.

**Fix:** wrap the fetch + stream in an `AbortController` with a timeout (aborting on inactivity), matching the C# feed's bounded timeout, so a hung feed surfaces the existing `ApiError` and clears `inFlight`.
