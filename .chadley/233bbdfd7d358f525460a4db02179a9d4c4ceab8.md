The `finishes` field is documented as "nonfoil, foil and/or etched" on both `Card` and `ArtVersion`, and it is carried through the whole stack. But the app has no path for etched anywhere:

- `FINISHES` offers only `all | nonfoil | foil`, and `priceFor` only reads `price.nonfoil` / `price.foil`.
- `matchesFinish(finishes, finish)` therefore filters an **etched-only** printing (e.g. many Commander Legends / Double Masters etched cards, `finishes: ["etched"]`) out of the grid under both the Nonfoil and Foil filters — it appears only under "All", where its price still shows a dash because no etched price is ever populated.
- The Scryfall mapper reads only `usd` and `usd_foil`; `ScryfallPrices` never declares `usd_etched`, so the etched market price Scryfall publishes is discarded on both the backend (`ScryfallCardMapper.TcgplayerPrice`) and the frontend (`tcgplayerPrices`).

This is a well-scoped enhancement the models already invite: add `etched` to `VendorPrice`, read `usd_etched`, add an Etched entry to `FINISHES`, and extend `priceFor`. Card Kingdom's feed likewise emits etched rows currently coerced into the foil slot.
