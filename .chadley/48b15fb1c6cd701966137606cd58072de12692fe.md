The `PeriodicTimer(RefreshInterval)` is constructed at the top of `ExecuteAsync`, *before* the initial refresh inside the `do` body runs. The first refresh downloads ~66 MB + ~50 MB catalogues and can take a meaningful amount of time. Because the timer's clock begins at construction rather than after the first refresh completes, the interval between the first download finishing and the first timer-driven refresh is shortened by however long the initial download took. In the pathological case where the first refresh exceeds `RefreshInterval`, `WaitForNextTickAsync` returns immediately and triggers a back-to-back re-download.

**Fix:** construct the `PeriodicTimer` after the first refresh completes, or run the initial refresh outside the loop and let `WaitForNextTickAsync` drive cadence, so each interval is measured from the completion of the previous refresh.
